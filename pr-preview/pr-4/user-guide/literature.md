# Literature and references

The terminology and methods used by `weightedstate` follow the literature on survival and multi-state analysis. Weighted Kaplan-Meier estimators are used in several settings, including inverse-probability weighting for causal inference. Keogh and Van Geloven (2024), for example, discuss weighted Kaplan-Meier curves after artificial censoring.


# References

Keogh, R. H., & Van Geloven, N. (2024). Weighted Kaplan-Meier curves for summarizing the effect of a time-varying treatment on a time-to-event outcome. *Statistics in Medicine*, 43(1), 3-20.
